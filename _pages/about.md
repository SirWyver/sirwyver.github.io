At Microsoft AI, I work on reinforcement learning for agentic models. Previously, I led research on 3D generative AI at Meta Reality Labs Zurich and helped bring photorealistic reconstruction to [Meta Horizon Hyperscape](https://www.meta.com/experiences/meta-horizon-hyperscape-demo/7972066712871980/).

I completed my PhD at the [Technical University of Munich](https://www.tum.de/en/), advised by [Prof. Matthias Niessner](https://niessnerlab.org/members/matthias_niessner/profile.html). My dissertation is [_Generative Models on 3D Representations_](https://mediatum.ub.tum.de/node?id=1759335&change_language=en). Before that, I earned Bachelor's and Master's degrees in both Computer Science and Mathematics at [RWTH Aachen University](https://www.rwth-aachen.de/go/id/a/), graduating at the top of my class.

My background also includes robotics at BMW and research internships with [Peter Kontschieder](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=CxbDDRMAAAAJ) at Meta. I build tools for research, including [DVIS](https://github.com/SirWyver/dvis) for browser-based 3D visualization.
